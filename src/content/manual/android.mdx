Makonome for Android is on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.makonome.app). This page is about version 1.3. It lists what the Android app can do and how it differs from Makonome on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## What the Android app has

- **The metronome:** a BPM dial with − and + buttons, tempo names, tap tempo, and beat rings you tap to accent or mute a beat. Any tempo from 20 to 400 BPM, and you can type it in.
- **Time signatures** from 1 to 16 beats over 2, 4, 8 or 16, accent groups like 2+2+3, and compound meters like 6/8 counted in dotted beats.
- **Subdivisions:** quarters, eighths, sixteenths, triplets, 32nds, quintuplets, sextuplets and septuplets, with rests inside the beat.
- **Count-in** of 1, 2 or 4 bars, with a click or a spoken count.
- **Auto stop** after a number of bars or a time, with a bar counter.
- **Screen flash** on the beat.
- **Sounds:** Click, Clave, Wood, Cowbell and Voice, with volume and pitch.
- **Visualizations:** Pendulum, Circular, Count and Line.
- **Gap Trainer**, by bars with a progressive mode or by seconds, and **Tempo Changer**, faster or slower, by bars or by time, with a target tempo.
- **Presets and collections**, with an artist, search, sorting, favorites and duplicates. You can drag presets and collections into your own order, and play a collection from the first preset to the last.
- **Practice tracking:** practice sessions with a time goal and a note, a weekly goal with a streak of weeks, history with a calendar and charts, and a daily practice reminder.
- **A home screen widget** with your practice time this week and your week streak. Tap it to open the Practice tab.
- **Tempo detection** and **haptic feedback**, as experimental features in Settings.
- **Fine-grained BPM** (steps of 0.5) and **Keep Screen On** in Settings.
- **Playback in the background**, with a notification to pause and play. A phone call pauses the metronome, and it plays again after the call.
- **Light, dark and system themes**, and layouts for landscape and tablets.
- **A link to this manual** in Settings.

Like on iPhone, there is a 14-day free trial with every feature open. It starts on first launch, with no account and no card. After it, the basic metronome stays free and the extra tools need Makonome Pro, a one-time purchase in Google Play. Pro on Android and Pro on iPhone, iPad and Mac are separate purchases.

Makonome for Android needs Android 10 or later.

## New in version 1.3

### Practice history

After a session, **How did it go?** asks for a note. Open a past session to see its details and edit the note, or tap **+** in History to add a session you forgot to record. History now has **List**, **Calendar** and **Charts**: the calendar shows the days you practiced, month by month, and the charts show your practice time by day, week, month or year. See [Practice history](/manual/practice-tracking/#practice-history).

Paused time no longer counts as practice, and **This Week** starts on your region's first day of the week.

### Weekly goal

Choose how many days a week to practice, with an optional daily minimum, and build a streak of weeks. This replaces the streak of days: **Streak** and **Best Streak** are gone from the Stats card, and the widget shows your week streak. The Daily Goal ring shows only when you set a daily minimum. See [Weekly goal and streak](/manual/practice-tracking/#weekly-goal-and-streak).

### Presets and collections

Presets can have an artist. Search presets by name or artist, and sort them by name, recently played or recently created. Drag presets and collections into your own order. Duplicate presets and collections, and make a collection a favorite. Rows show muted and accented beats, and you can tick several presets at once when you add them to a collection. Loading a preset no longer resets your volume and pitch. See [Presets and collections](/manual/presets-and-collections/).

Editing a preset now needs Pro.

### Trainers

The Tempo Changer can go slower as well as faster, and at the target it can stay, start over or bounce back. The Gap Trainer has a Timed mode: a set number of seconds on, then a set number of seconds off. During a gap the visuals and haptics stop as well as the click. Trainer settings now stay after a restart. See [Trainers](/manual/trainers/).

### Half-step BPM

With **Fine-grained BPM** on, the dial and the − and + buttons step by 0.5, and the dial, presets and notification show the half step. See [Set the tempo](/manual/metronome-screen/#set-the-tempo).

## New in version 1.2

### Quintuplets, sextuplets, septuplets and rests

The subdivision buttons in the **Time Signature** sheet are now two rows of larger buttons. The second row has **Quintuplets**, **Sextuplets** and **Septuplets**: 5, 6 or 7 notes in each beat. Under the buttons you can tap single notes to make them rests. Compound meters like 6/8 counted in dotted beats keep the subdivisions they had, without the new ones and without rests. See [Subdivisions](/manual/time-signatures/#subdivisions) and [Rests inside the beat](/manual/time-signatures/#rests-inside-the-beat).

A muted beat now silences its subdivision notes too.

### Haptic feedback at every tempo

Haptic feedback now works with subdivisions at every tempo. Before, it stopped at fast tempos, for example above 75 BPM with 16ths.

### Headphones

If you unplug your headphones, or a Bluetooth device or USB audio interface disconnects, the metronome stops. It doesn't play on through the phone's speaker.

### Chromebooks and desktop windows

On a Chromebook or in a desktop window, Makonome opens in its own window and works with a mouse and a keyboard:

- Turn the scroll wheel over the dial to change the tempo.
- Right-click a preset or a collection for its menu.
- <kbd>Space</kbd> plays and stops. <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> change the tempo, with <kbd>Shift</kbd> in steps of 5. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes a sheet.

### Notifications

On Android 13 and later, the first time you tap play, Makonome asks to show notifications. With them on, the playback controls show in the notification shade. The metronome starts right away, whatever you answer.

## New in version 1.1

### Count-in and auto stop

On the metronome screen, tap **•••**, then **Count-in** or **Auto Stop**. The **Playback Options** sheet opens. Both are free, and they are not part of presets.

- **Count-in (bars):** **Off**, **1**, **2** or **4**. The **Sound** is **Click** (your metronome sound) or **Voice** (a spoken count). The count-in clicks once a beat, with your accents.
- **Auto Stop:** **Off**, **Bars** (1 to 999 bars) or **Time** (10 seconds to 60 minutes). It stops at the end of the last bar, or on the first beat after the time. The count-in doesn't count. Bars that the Gap Trainer mutes do.

While auto stop runs, the readout under the beat rings counts along: **Bar 3 of 10, Beat 2** for bars, or **Bar 3, Beat 2 · 0:42 left** for a time.

### Screen flash

Tap **••• → Screen Flash** to turn it on or off. It is free. The flash comes on the beat, at most 3 times a second: up to 180 beats a minute every beat flashes, faster than that only the first beat of each bar, and with very fast short bars it doesn't flash at all.

If flashing light is a problem for you, leave screen flash off.

### Tempo from 20 BPM, and typing it

The tempo now goes from 20 to 400 BPM. Tap the BPM number in the middle of the dial to type a tempo. Tap tempo works like on iPhone: tap along, and Makonome sets the tempo.

### Play a collection

<Pro /> Playing a collection needs Makonome Pro. Open a collection and tap **Play Collection**. The metronome screen shows a player bar with the collection name, the preset name and its place, for example **2 of 5**, a back and a forward button, and a close button that ends the collection.

You can switch presets without touching the screen. A keyboard or a Bluetooth page-turner foot pedal works too:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> | Previous preset |
| <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Page Down</kbd> | Next preset |

Holding a key down moves one preset only.

### Reorder a collection

Inside a collection, drag a preset by its handle to move it. Reordering is free. With TalkBack, use the **Move up** and **Move down** actions.

### Haptic feedback

<Experimental feature="Haptic Feedback" where="Settings → Experimental" />

<Pro /> Haptic feedback needs Makonome Pro. With haptic feedback on, your phone vibrates on each beat.

1. Open **Settings → Experimental** and switch on **Haptic Feedback**.
2. On the metronome screen, tap **••• → Haptic Feedback** to turn the vibration on or off.

On a device that can't vibrate, nothing happens. It uses Android's touch feedback setting: if touch feedback is off in your phone's vibration settings, the beat doesn't vibrate.

### Phone calls

A phone call pauses the metronome, and it starts again by itself after the call. An alarm or a voice assistant does the same. When another app starts playing music, Makonome stops.

### User Manual

**Settings → Help → User Manual** opens this manual in a browser tab.

## What's different from iPhone, iPad and Mac

- **One widget:** one home screen widget. No lock screen widgets.
- **Practice goals:** a session goal goes from 5 to 120 minutes, and with a daily minimum set, the Practice tab has a daily goal ring.
- **Starting a session:** you can't pick a preset when you start a session. The session records the preset that is loaded.
- **No iCloud.** There is no iCloud backup on Android. Android's own backup keeps your presets, collections, practice history and settings.
