Makonome is a metronome for practice. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. This page shows you how to get it, what is free, and how to play your first click.

## Install Makonome

- **iPhone and iPad:** get Makonome from the App Store.
- **Mac:** get Makonome from the Mac App Store. It is the same app, made for the Mac, with its own window, a menu bar menu and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Android:** get Makonome from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.makonome.app). See [Makonome for Android](/manual/android/) for how it differs.

## The free trial and Pro

When you open Makonome for the first time, a **14-day free trial** starts. During the trial every feature is open.

After the trial, the basic metronome stays free. The extra tools need **Makonome Pro**. Pro is a one-time purchase with lifetime access. There is no subscription.

After the trial, features that need Pro show a small sparkle icon<Platform only="android" inline> or a **PRO** badge</Platform>. Tap one to see the upgrade screen.

<Platform only="ios mac">
To check your trial or buy Pro, open **Settings → Pro**. It shows how many days are left in your trial, **Upgrade to Pro** and **Restore Purchases**. On a new device, use Restore Purchases to unlock Pro again without paying twice. Reinstalling the app does not restart the trial.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
To check your trial or buy Pro, look at the **Makonome Pro** card at the top of Settings. It shows how many days are left in your trial, **Upgrade to Pro** and **Restore**. On a new device, use Restore to unlock Pro again without paying twice. Pro on Android is a purchase in Google Play, separate from Pro on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
</Platform>

### What's free

- The metronome: 20 to 400 BPM, the dial, typing a tempo and tap tempo
- All time signatures, accent groups and subdivisions, with rests inside the beat
- Accents and muted beats
- The Click sound and the volume
- Count-in, auto stop and screen flash
- Loading, favoriting and deleting presets and collections
- Practice history, stats, streaks, reminders and widgets<Platform only="ios mac" inline>, and iCloud backup</Platform>
- Visualizations<Platform only="ios mac" inline> and Ableton Link (both experimental, see below)</Platform>

## The three tabs

Makonome has three tabs:

- **Metronome:** the dial, the beats and the play button. See [The metronome screen](/manual/metronome-screen/).
- **Practice:** timed practice sessions, history and streaks. See [Practice tracking](/manual/practice-tracking/).
- **Settings:** theme, reminders, experimental features, <Platform only="ios mac" inline>backup, </Platform>help and feedback. See [Settings, backup and data](/manual/settings-and-data/).

<Platform only="ios">
On iPhone the tabs are at the bottom of the screen. On iPad they are at the top.
</Platform>

<Platform only="mac">
The tabs are at the top of the window.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On a phone the tabs are at the bottom of the screen. In a wide window, like a tablet in landscape, they move to a bar on the left.
</Platform>

## Your first click in 30 seconds

1. Open the **Metronome** tab.
2. Drag around the dial to set the tempo. Or tap the number and type a tempo.
3. Press the green **play** button.
4. Press it again to stop.

<Screenshot name="metronome-main" alt="The Metronome tab playing at 120 BPM, Allegro, with the readout Bar 2, Beat 3 under the beat rings" caption="The metronome playing in 4/4." />

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac, press <kbd>Space</kbd> to start and stop. Press <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> to change the tempo by one step. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to change it by 5.
</Platform>

To change the time signature, tap <Platform only="ios mac" inline>the button with the notes, second from the left</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>the button that shows the meter, for example **4/4**, right of the play button</Platform>. See [Time signatures](/manual/time-signatures/).

## Experimental features

Some features are hidden until you turn them on. Some are still new, and some are for special setups. All of them are **off** at first.

<Platform only="ios mac">
To turn one on, open **Settings → Experimental Features** and switch the feature on.

<Screenshot name="settings-experimental" alt="Settings, Experimental Features: Haptic Feedback, Tempo Detection, Accuracy Trainer, Visualizations, Fine-grained BPM and Ableton Link, each with a switch" caption="Settings → Experimental Features." />

| Feature | What it does when on |
|---|---|
| **Haptic Feedback** <Pro /> | Adds Haptic Feedback to the ••• menu on the metronome screen. iPhone and iPad only. See [Haptic feedback](/manual/sound-and-feel/#haptic-feedback). |
| **Tempo Detection** <Pro /> | Adds Tempo Detection to the ••• menu. See [Tempo detection](/manual/tempo-detection/). |
| **Accuracy Trainer** <Pro /> | Adds the Accuracy Trainer to the ••• menu. See [Accuracy Trainer](/manual/accuracy-trainer/). |
| **Visualizations** | Adds a visualization button to the top left of the metronome screen. See [Visualizations](/manual/sound-and-feel/#visualizations). |
| **Fine-grained BPM** | Lets you set the tempo in steps of 0.5 BPM. |
| **Ableton Link** | Adds a Link button to the top left of the metronome screen. You turn Link itself on from there. See [Ableton Link](/manual/ableton-link/). |
</Platform>

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac there is no Haptic Feedback switch.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android the section is called **Settings → Experimental**, and it has three switches:

| Feature | What it does when on |
|---|---|
| **Haptic Feedback** <Pro /> | Adds Haptic Feedback to the ⋮ menu on the metronome screen. See [Haptic feedback](/manual/sound-and-feel/#haptic-feedback). |
| **Tempo Detection** <Pro /> | Adds Tempo Detection to the ⋮ menu. See [Tempo detection](/manual/tempo-detection/). |
| **Fine-grained BPM** | Lets you type a tempo like 120.5 (not in dotted meters like 6/8), and lets tap tempo and the Tempo Changer use steps of 0.5 BPM. |

Visualizations are not experimental on Android: their button is always in the top left of the metronome screen. The Accuracy Trainer and Ableton Link are not in the Android app yet.
</Platform>
