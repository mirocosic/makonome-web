## Your first click

Open Makonome. The Metronome tab shows the BPM dial. Press the green play button to start.

<Screenshot name="metronome-main" alt="The Metronome tab with the BPM dial at 173 BPM, Vivace" caption="The metronome screen." />

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac, press <kbd>Space</kbd> to start and stop, and the arrow keys to change the tempo.
</Platform>

<Platform only="ios android">
Tap the play button at the bottom of the screen to start and stop.
</Platform>

## Pick a time signature

Tap the time signature to open the meter picker.
