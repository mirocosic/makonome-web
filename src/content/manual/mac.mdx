Makonome on Mac is the same app as on iPhone and iPad, made for the Mac. Everything in this manual works the same way, with a few differences on this page: the window, the menu bar, keyboard shortcuts, and a few features that only make sense on a phone.

Get it from the Mac App Store. If you already bought Pro on iPhone or iPad, open **Settings → Pro → Restore Purchases** on the Mac to unlock it there too.

## The window

The tabs **Metronome**, **Practice** and **Settings** are at the top of the window. You can make the window bigger or smaller, from 400 × 600 up to 800 × 1200 points.

<Screenshot name="metronome-main" alt="The Makonome window on Mac playing at 120 BPM in 4/4, with the tabs at the top, the beat rings, the dial and the play button" only="mac" caption="The Makonome window." />

The window follows **Settings → Appearance → Theme**: Light, Dark, or System (the same as macOS).

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Play or stop |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> | Tempo up by one step |
| <kbd>↓</kbd> | Tempo down by one step |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>↑</kbd> | Tempo up by 5 |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>↓</kbd> | Tempo down by 5 |
| <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> | Previous preset, while a collection plays |
| <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Page Down</kbd> | Next preset, while a collection plays |

One step is 1 BPM, or 0.5 BPM with **Fine-grained BPM** on in Settings → Experimental Features.

## The Metronome menu

The menu bar has a **Metronome** menu with the same commands:

- **Play / Stop**
- **Increase Tempo** and **Decrease Tempo**
- **Increase Tempo by 5** and **Decrease Tempo by 5**
- **Previous Preset**, **Next Preset** and **Stop Playing Collection**. These are on only while a [collection plays](/manual/presets-and-collections/#play-a-collection).

## Foot pedals

Bluetooth page-turner foot pedals work on Mac. Most of them send the arrow keys or Page Up and Page Down, like a keyboard. While a collection plays, a pedal moves to the previous or next preset. Pair the pedal in **System Settings → Bluetooth** first. See [Keys and foot pedals](/manual/presets-and-collections/#keys-and-foot-pedals).

## Sheets have a close button

On iPhone you close a sheet by swiping it down. On Mac, sheets like **Time Signature**, **Playback Options** and **Tempo Detection** have a **✕** button in the top right corner. Click it to close the sheet.

## The Accuracy Trainer window

The [Accuracy Trainer](/manual/accuracy-trainer/) opens in its own window, so you can use it next to the metronome. Open it with **••• → Accuracy Trainer** on the metronome screen. If it is already open, this brings it to the front.

You can resize the trainer window. When it is wide, it shows the controls on the left and your timing on the right, with a histogram and a beat map for each drum.

<Screenshot name="trainer-live" alt="The Accuracy Trainer window on Mac: the controls on the left, and on the right the rush and drag needle and a list of drums with their timing charts" only="mac" caption="The Accuracy Trainer in its own window." />

USB and Bluetooth MIDI drum kits work on Mac. Pair a Bluetooth MIDI device in **Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio → Bluetooth**.

## Widgets

The **Practice Stats** widget comes in two sizes on Mac: small and medium. To add one, right-click the desktop, pick **Edit Widgets**, find **Makonome**, and drag the widget to the desktop or to Notification Center. See [Widgets](/manual/practice-tracking/#widgets).

## iCloud on Mac

The Mac app has its own [iCloud backup](/manual/settings-and-data/#icloud-backup-and-restore), like the iPhone and iPad app. It is a backup, not a sync: presets and practice sessions you make on the Mac don't show up on your iPhone while both are in use. All your devices with the same Apple ID back up to the same place, so the backup holds what the device that backed up last had. A backup is restored only when you install Makonome fresh.

## Ableton Link on Mac

[Ableton Link](/manual/ableton-link/) works on Mac. macOS may ask to let Makonome find devices on your local network, and a firewall may ask about incoming connections. Allow both, or Link can't find anyone.

## Not on Mac

- **Haptic feedback.** Macs can't tap you on the beat, so there is no Haptic Feedback switch.
- **Lock Screen widgets.** The Mac has the small and medium widgets only.
- **Home Screen quick actions.** These are for iPhone and iPad.

The User Manual in **Settings → Help** opens in your web browser on Mac.
