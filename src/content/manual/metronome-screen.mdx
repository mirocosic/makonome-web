The Metronome tab is where you set the tempo, start and stop, and choose which beats are accented or muted.

<Screenshot name="metronome-idle" alt="The Metronome tab stopped at 120 BPM in 4/4: four beat rings at the top, the label Quarter Notes and 4/4, the BPM dial, and five buttons at the bottom" caption="The metronome screen, stopped." />

From top to bottom:

<Platform only="ios mac">
- **Top bar:** the Presets button on the right. The Visualization and Ableton Link buttons appear on the left when you turn them on in [Experimental Features](/manual/getting-started/#experimental-features).
- **Beat rings:** one ring for each beat of the bar. Tap a ring to accent or mute that beat.
- **Bar and beat readout:** where you are in the bar, while the metronome plays.
- **Settings row:** the subdivision and the time signature, for example "♩ Quarter Notes · 4/4".
- **The BPM dial:** the tempo, with − and + buttons and the tempo name.
- **Bottom buttons:** tap tempo, time signature, play/stop, sound, and the ••• menu.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
- **Top bar:** the Visualization button on the left, always there, and the Presets button on the right.
- **Beat rings:** one ring for each beat of the bar. Tap a ring to accent or mute that beat.
- **Meter label:** the time signature and the subdivision, for example "4/4 ♩ Quarter Notes". Tap it to open the Time Signature sheet. While the metronome plays, the bar and beat readout takes its place.
- **The BPM dial:** the tempo, with − and + buttons and the tempo name.
- **Bottom buttons:** tap tempo, sound, play/stop, time signature (it shows the meter, for example **4/4**), and the ⋮ menu.

In a wide window, like a tablet in landscape, the screen splits into two columns.
</Platform>

## Set the tempo

The tempo goes from **20 to 400 BPM**.

To set the tempo, do one of these:

- **Drag around the dial.** Drag clockwise to go faster and counterclockwise to go slower. The full range takes about three turns.
- **Tap − or +** next to the number. Each tap changes the tempo by 1 BPM.

<Platform only="ios">
On iPhone and iPad you feel a light tick as the tempo changes.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
You feel a light tick as the tempo changes, if touch feedback is on in your phone's sound and vibration settings.
</Platform>

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac, press <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> to change the tempo by one step. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to change it by 5. The same commands are in the **Metronome** menu in the menu bar.
</Platform>

The tempo steps are whole numbers. <Platform only="ios mac" inline>To use steps of 0.5 BPM, turn on **Fine-grained BPM** in **Settings → Experimental Features**.</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
**Fine-grained BPM** in **Settings → Experimental** lets you type a tempo like 120.5, and lets tap tempo and the Tempo Changer use steps of 0.5 BPM. The dial and the − and + buttons still move in whole steps, and the dial shows the whole number.
</Platform>

In compound meters like 6/8, the dial shows the tempo of the dotted beat. See [Compound meters](/manual/time-signatures/#compound-meters-and-the-dotted-pulse).

## Type a tempo

1. Tap the tempo number in the middle of the dial.
2. Type the tempo.
3. Tap <Platform only="ios mac" inline>**Done** (or press <kbd>Return</kbd> on a keyboard)</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>**Set**</Platform>.

If the number is outside the range, the tempo stays as it was.

<Screenshot name="metronome-bpm-typing" alt="The tempo number 120 being edited, with a number pad and a Done button below the dial" only="ios" caption="Typing a tempo on iPhone." />

<Screenshot name="metronome-bpm-typing" alt="The Mac window with the tempo number 120 selected for typing in the middle of the dial" only="mac" caption="Typing a tempo on Mac." />

<Platform only="ios">
The number pad has no decimal point, so you can only type whole numbers. With Fine-grained BPM on, use − and + for the half steps.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android a **Set Tempo** box opens. If the number is outside the range, it says **Enter a BPM from 20 to 400** and stays open, so you can fix the number. With Fine-grained BPM on, you can type a decimal, like 120.5.
</Platform>

## Tap tempo

Use tap tempo to set the tempo by feel, for example to match a song.

1. Tap the hand button (bottom left) in time with the music.
2. Keep tapping. Every tap makes the tempo more exact.
3. Stop tapping. The tempo stays.

Tap tempo counts a tap the moment your finger touches the screen. It ignores an accidental double tap and one stray tap. If you miss a beat, it still works. If you change speed while tapping, it follows the new tempo. If you pause for a while, the next tap starts over.

## Start and stop

Press the green **play** button to start. It turns into a **stop** button while the metronome plays.

The metronome keeps playing when you switch tabs, leave the app or lock the screen. It also works with Bluetooth speakers and headphones.

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac, press <kbd>Space</kbd> to start and stop, or use **Metronome → Play / Stop** in the menu bar.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
While the metronome plays, Android shows a Makonome notification with **Pause** and **Stop**. Use it to stop the metronome from another app or the lock screen.
</Platform>

## Beat accents and muting

Each beat ring has three states. Tap a ring to change it, in this order:

1. **Normal:** an empty ring. A regular click.
2. **Accented:** <Platform only="ios mac" inline>a filled ring</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>a ring with a filled dot inside</Platform>. A stronger click.
3. **Muted:** a dashed ring. No click on that beat.

The next tap goes back to normal.

<Screenshot name="metronome-beat-states" alt="Four beat rings: beat 1 and beat 3 filled (accented), beat 2 empty (normal), beat 4 dashed (muted)" only="ios mac" caption="Beats 1 and 3 accented, beat 2 normal, beat 4 muted." />

Use accents to hear where the bar starts or how the beats are grouped. Use muted beats to practice keeping time on your own, for example on beats 2 and 4.

While the metronome plays, the current beat lights up. With more than eight beats, the rings wrap to a second row. In meters like 7/8, small gaps show the beat groups. To set the accents for a group like 2+2+3 in one tap, use [accent groups](/manual/time-signatures/#accent-groups).

## The bar and beat readout

While the metronome plays, the line under the beat rings shows where you are:

- **Bar 3, Beat 2:** the usual readout.
- **Bar 3 of 10, Beat 2:** with [auto stop](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/#auto-stop) set to 10 bars.
- **Bar 3, Beat 2 · 0:42 left:** with auto stop set to a time.
- **Count-in 2 of 4, Beat 3:** during a [count-in](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/#count-in) of more than one bar. A one-bar count-in shows **Count-in, Beat 3**.

<Screenshot name="metronome-main" alt="The metronome playing at 120 BPM, with beat 3 lit and the readout Bar 2, Beat 3" only="mac" caption="The readout on Mac." />

When Gap Trainer or Tempo Changer is on, a second line under the readout shows its state. <Platform only="ios mac" inline>The settings row also shows **Gap** or **Tempo+**.</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>On Android the line shows only while the metronome plays, and the ⋮ button is highlighted while a trainer is on.</Platform> See [Trainers](/manual/trainers/).

## The More menu

<Platform only="ios mac">
The More button (•••, bottom right) opens a menu with more options. Each entry shows whether it is on.

<Screenshot name="metronome-more-menu" alt="The ••• menu open: Gap Trainer, Tempo Changer, Count-in, Auto Stop and Screen Flash, then Haptic Feedback, Tempo Detection and Accuracy Trainer" only="ios" caption="The ••• menu, with all experimental features turned on." />

| Entry | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Gap Trainer** <Pro /> | Mutes whole bars so you practice keeping time. See [Gap Trainer](/manual/trainers/#gap-trainer). |
| **Tempo Changer** <Pro /> | Raises the tempo step by step. See [Tempo Changer](/manual/trainers/#tempo-changer). |
| **Count-in** | Plays bars before the music starts. See [Count-in](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/#count-in). |
| **Auto Stop** | Stops after a number of bars or a time. See [Auto stop](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/#auto-stop). |
| **Screen Flash** | Turns the screen flash on or off. See [Screen flash](/manual/sound-and-feel/#screen-flash). |
| **Haptic Feedback** <Pro /> | Turns beat vibrations on or off. Experimental, iPhone and iPad only. See [Haptic feedback](/manual/sound-and-feel/#haptic-feedback). |
| **Tempo Detection** <Pro /> | Listens through the microphone and finds the tempo. Experimental. See [Tempo detection](/manual/tempo-detection/). |
| **Accuracy Trainer** <Pro /> | Measures your drum timing over MIDI. Experimental. See [Accuracy Trainer](/manual/accuracy-trainer/). |

The last three entries appear only after you turn them on in **Settings → Experimental Features**.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android the button is ⋮ (**More options**), and the menu has, in this order: **Tempo Changer**, **Gap Trainer**, **Count-in**, **Auto Stop**, **Screen Flash**, then **Haptic Feedback** and **Tempo Detection** when you turn them on in **Settings → Experimental**. Count-in, Auto Stop and Screen Flash show their setting, for example **Count-in (2 bars)**. The others show a colored icon when they are on. There is no Accuracy Trainer.

See [Trainers](/manual/trainers/), [Count-in and auto stop](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/), [Screen flash](/manual/sound-and-feel/#screen-flash), [Haptic feedback](/manual/sound-and-feel/#haptic-feedback) and [Tempo detection](/manual/tempo-detection/).
</Platform>

The other bottom buttons open [Time signatures](/manual/time-signatures/) (<Platform only="ios mac" inline>the notes button</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>the button that shows the meter</Platform>) and [Sound Settings](/manual/sound-and-feel/) (the speaker button). The Presets button in the top bar opens [Presets and collections](/manual/presets-and-collections/).
