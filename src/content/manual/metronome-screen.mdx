## Basic controls

The main screen shows a circular BPM dial. Drag around the dial to adjust tempo, or tap the center to type a specific BPM value.

- **BPM Range:** 40 to 400 BPM
- **Tap Tempo:** Tap the hand icon repeatedly to set tempo by feel
- **Play/Stop:** The large green button starts and stops the metronome

## Beat accents and muting

Tap any beat indicator at the top to cycle through states:

- **Normal:** Regular click sound
- **Accented:** Louder, higher-pitched click (filled circle)
- **Muted:** Silent beat (dashed circle)

Use this to create custom accent patterns or practice specific rhythmic figures.
