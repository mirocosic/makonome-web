Makonome can time your practice. You start a **practice session** when you begin, and complete it when you are done. Makonome keeps a history of your sessions, counts your streak of practice days, and shows your week on a widget.

Sessions are not recorded by themselves: only the sessions you start are tracked.

<Pro /> Starting a practice session needs Makonome Pro. Your history, stats, streaks, reminders and widgets are free, so you keep seeing them after the trial.

## Start a practice session

1. Open the **Practice** tab.
2. Tap **Start Practice Session**.
3. In **New Session**, type a name, or keep "Practice Session".
4. To set a goal, turn on **Set time goal** and pick a **Target Duration** from 1 to 60 minutes.
5. To start the metronome with the session, turn on **Start metronome**. You can then pick a **Preset** to load, or keep **None** to play the current settings.
6. Tap **Start**.

The **Start metronome** switch is on at first if **Settings → Practice Integration → Auto-start metronome** is on.

Starting a session ends a [playing collection](/manual/presets-and-collections/#play-a-collection). If you pick a preset, it loads before the metronome starts.

<Platform only="ios">
You can also start a session from the Home Screen: touch and hold the Makonome icon and pick **Start Practice**. It opens the Practice tab and starts a session at once, with the name "Practice Session" and no goal. The metronome starts with it if **Auto-start metronome** is on in Settings.
</Platform>

## During a session

The Practice tab shows the session name, the time so far, and, with a goal, a progress bar with **Goal: 30:00** and the time remaining. When you reach the goal it shows **Goal Met!**. The session keeps running until you complete it.

While a session runs, the Practice tab button shows the time.

- **Pause:** tap the pause button. The timer stops. If the session started the metronome, the metronome stops too.
- **Resume:** tap the play button. The timer goes on, and the metronome starts again (with its [count-in](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/), if you set one).
- **Complete:** tap **Complete Session**. The session goes into your history. If the session started the metronome, it stops.

<Screenshot name="practice-active" alt="A running practice session called Groove work at 12:36, with a progress bar, Goal: 30:00 and 17:23 remaining, a pause button and a Complete Session button, and the Practice tab button showing 12:36" only="ios" caption="A session with a 30-minute goal." />

You can use the metronome tab freely during a session. [Auto stop](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/#auto-stop) stops only the metronome: the session keeps timing until you complete it.

## Practice history

To see your past sessions, tap the clock button in the top right corner of the Practice tab. **Practice History** opens.

- Pick **All Time**, **This Week** or **This Month** at the top.
- The summary shows the number of sessions, the total time, the average, and **Goals met: 11/20** (sessions that reached their goal, out of sessions with a goal).
- Each row shows the name, the length, the date and time, and the goal. A green check means the goal was met.
- To delete a session, swipe left on it, or touch and hold it and pick **Delete** (right-click on Mac).

<Screenshot name="practice-history" alt="Practice History with All Time selected: 20 Sessions, Average 30:42, 10:14:00 Total Time, Goals met 11/20, and a list of sessions such as Warm-up and rudiments, 32:00, 22 October 2026, with a 30:00 goal and a green check" caption="Practice History, all time." />

History shows how long you practiced. It does not save the tempo or the metronome settings of a session.

**This Week** means the last 7 days, and **This Month** the last month, counted back from now.

## Stats and streaks

The Practice tab shows, when no session runs:

- **Sessions:** all completed sessions
- **Total Time:** all your practice time
- **This Week:** your practice time in the last 7 days
- **Streak:** how many days in a row you practiced, with your best streak, for example **6 day streak (best: 9)**

A day counts for the streak when you complete at least one session that day. If you practiced yesterday, the streak is still alive today: practice today to keep it going. If you miss a whole day, the streak starts again. Your best streak is kept.

## Practice reminders

Makonome can remind you to practice every day.

1. Open **Settings → Practice Reminders**.
2. Turn on **Daily practice reminder**. Allow notifications when asked.
3. Set the **Reminder time**.

Every day at that time you get a notification, **Time to Practice!**.

If notifications are off for Makonome, Settings shows **Notifications disabled**, with a button to open the system settings and turn them on.

## Widgets

The **Practice Stats** widget shows your practice this week and your streak. It is free. Tap it to open the Practice tab.

| Widget | Shows |
|---|---|
| Small | This week's time and your streak |
| Medium | This week's time, the number of sessions and your streak |

<Platform only="ios">
To add a widget on iPhone or iPad: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit → Add Widget** (or **+**), find **Makonome**, pick a size and tap **Add Widget**.

There are also three **Lock Screen** widgets:

| Widget | Shows |
|---|---|
| Circular | Your streak, with a flame |
| Rectangular | This Week, the time, and the sessions and streak |
| Inline (above the clock) | The time this week, for example "2h 5m this week" |

To add one, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customize**, pick the Lock Screen, and tap the widget area.

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac there are the small and medium widgets. To add one, right-click the desktop, pick **Edit Widgets**, find **Makonome** and drag the widget to the desktop or Notification Center.
</Platform>

The widget updates when you complete a session, and at least every 30 minutes and at midnight. The widget's **This Week** is the calendar week (it starts on the first day of the week in your region), so on the first day of a week it starts at 0. The Practice tab counts the last 7 days, so the two numbers can differ.

### The widget shows 0

After you update to Makonome 1.1, **open the app once**. Until 1.1 the widget could not read your practice data. The first launch of 1.1 moves your data to where the widget can read it. Until then the widget shows zeros.

## Back up your practice

Your sessions, stats and streaks can be backed up to iCloud with the rest of your Makonome data, and come back when you reinstall the app. Turn it on in **Settings → Data Management → Back up to iCloud**. See [Settings, backup and data](/manual/settings-and-data/).
