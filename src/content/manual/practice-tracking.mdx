Makonome can time your practice. You start a **practice session** when you begin, and complete it when you are done. You can add a note, look back at your sessions in a list, a calendar or charts, set a **weekly goal** and build a streak of weeks, and see your week on a widget.

Sessions are not recorded by themselves: only the sessions you start are tracked.

<Pro /> Starting a practice session, adding a past session, changing your weekly goal and editing a saved note need Makonome Pro. Your history, calendar, charts, stats, streak, reminders and widgets are free, so you keep seeing them after the trial. You can also delete sessions without Pro.

## Start a practice session

1. Open the **Practice** tab.
2. Tap **Start Practice Session**.

<Platform only="ios mac">
3. In **New Session**, type a name, or keep "Practice Session".
4. To set a goal, turn on **Set time goal** and pick a **Target Duration** from 1 to 60 minutes.
5. To start the metronome with the session, turn on **Start metronome**. You can then pick a **Preset** to load, or keep **None** to play the current settings.
6. Tap **Start**.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
3. Type a **Session Name**, or keep the one Makonome suggests, for example "Practice Session #4".
4. To set a goal, turn on **Set a time goal** and pick 5 to 120 minutes, in steps of 5.
5. To start the metronome with the session, turn on **Auto-start metronome**. The metronome plays your current settings: there is no preset choice.
6. Tap **Start**.
</Platform>

## During a session

<Platform only="ios mac">
The Practice tab shows the session name, the time so far, and, with a goal, a progress bar with **Goal: 30:00** and the time remaining. When you reach the goal it shows **Goal Met!**. The session keeps running until you complete it.

While a session runs, the Practice tab button shows the time.

- **Pause:** tap the pause button. The timer stops. If the session started the metronome, the metronome stops too.
- **Resume:** tap the play button. The timer goes on, and the metronome starts again (with its [count-in](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/), if you set one).
- **Complete:** tap **Complete Session**. The session goes into your history. If the session started the metronome, it stops.

Then **How did it go?** asks for a note. Type one and tap **Save**, or tap **Skip**. The note is optional.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
The Practice tab shows the session name, the time so far, and, with a goal, a progress bar with the time remaining. When you reach the goal it shows **Goal met!**. The session keeps running until you complete it. You can type **Notes** about the session. They are saved in your history.

- **Pause:** tap the pause button. If the metronome is playing, it stops.
- **Resume:** tap the play button. The metronome starts too, even if it wasn't playing before the pause (with its [count-in](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/), if you set one).
- **Complete:** tap **Complete**. The session goes into your history. If the metronome is playing, it stops.

Then **How did it go?** asks for a note. It starts with what you typed in **Notes**. Tap **Save**, or tap **Skip**.

Paused time doesn't count as practice. A running session isn't saved until you complete it: if you close Makonome completely, it is lost.
</Platform>

You can use the metronome tab freely during a session. [Auto stop](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/#auto-stop) stops only the metronome: the session keeps timing until you complete it.

## Practice history

To see your past sessions, tap the clock button in the top right corner of the Practice tab. **<Platform only="ios mac" inline>Practice History</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>History</Platform>** opens. Use the switch at the top to go between **List**, **Calendar** and **Charts**. Makonome remembers your choice.

### List

<Platform only="ios mac">
- Pick **All Time**, **This Week** or **This Month** at the top.
- The summary shows the number of sessions, the total time, the average, and **Goals met: 11/20** (sessions that reached their goal, out of sessions with a goal).
- Each row shows the name, the length, the date and time, and the goal. A green check means the goal was met.
- To delete a session, swipe left on it, or touch and hold it and pick **Delete** (right-click on Mac).
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
- Pick **All Time**, **This Week** or **This Month** at the top.
- The summary shows **Sessions**, **Total Time**, **Average** and **Goals Met** (the number of sessions that reached their goal).
- Each row shows the name, the length and the goal, the date and time, the preset and your note. A green check means the goal was met.
- To delete a session, swipe left on it and tap the delete button.
</Platform>

<Screenshot name="practice-history" alt="Practice History with List selected and All Time under it: the number of sessions, the average, the total time, Goals met, and a list of sessions with their length, date, goal and a green check" caption="Practice History, all time." />

**This Week** is the calendar week. It starts on the first day of the week in your region, so on that day it starts at 0. **This Month** is <Platform only="ios mac" inline>the last month, counted back from today</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>the current calendar month</Platform>.

### Session details

Tap a session to open it. You see the date, the start and end time, the duration and the preset, whether you met your goal, and your note.

- **Edit the note:** tap the **Note** field and type. It saves when you leave the field. Editing a note needs Pro. Without Pro you see the note, or **No note**, and **Edit with Pro**.
- **Delete the session:** tap **Delete Session**. It asks first. This is free.

A session saves how long you practiced and the preset. It does not save the tempo or the other metronome settings.

### Add a past session

<Pro /> Forgot to start a session? Add it afterwards.

1. In **<Platform only="ios mac" inline>Practice History</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>History</Platform>**, tap **+**.
2. Set when it **Started**.
3. Set the **Duration**, from 1 minute to 12 hours. It is 30 minutes at first.
4. Pick a **Preset** if you like, and add a **Note**.
5. Tap **Save**.

The session is called after its preset, or "Practice" without one. It shows an **Added** tag, and it counts for your calendar, charts, weekly goal and streak. It can't end in the future. An added session has no time goal. With Pro you can still edit it later.

### Calendar

The calendar shows a month at a time, on today's month at first. A filled day has at least one finished session, and today has a ring. The header shows the month and its total, for example **12 days · 6 h 40 min**.

- Tap a day to see its sessions. A day without any shows **No practice**. Tap a session to open it.
- Swipe, or use **‹** and **›**, to change the month. You can go back to the month of your first session.

A session counts on the day it ended, so one that runs past midnight counts on the new day.

### Charts

Charts show your practice time as bars. Each bar is the total time in that period. Pick the range at the top:

| Range | Bars |
|---|---|
| **Day** | The last 14 days |
| **Week** | The last 12 weeks. This is the range at first |
| **Month** | The last 12 months |
| **Year** | One bar per year, from your first session |

The header shows the total and the average, for example **Total 6 h 40 min · Avg 29 min/day**. The average counts the days or weeks you didn't practice too. Tap a bar to see its date and time. Tap it again to clear it.

## Weekly goal and streak

The weekly goal is the number of days a week you want to practice. Every week you reach it adds to your streak.

The goal card on the Practice tab shows how far you are this week, with a dot for each day:

- **2 more days to reach your goal**, then **Goal reached this week**.
- Below that, your streak: **Goal reached 4 weeks in a row · Best: 9 weeks**.
- With no streak yet: **Reach 3 days this week to start a streak**.

<Screenshot name="practice-goal" alt="The weekly goal card on the Practice tab: Goal reached this week, a dot for each day, and the week streak" only="ios" caption="The weekly goal card." />

<Screenshot name="practice-goal" alt="The weekly goal card on Android with a dot for each day of the week and the week streak" only="android" caption="The weekly goal on Android." />

To set your goal, tap the goal card<Platform only="android" inline> (or **Set a Weekly Goal** on the Practice tab)</Platform>. **Weekly Goal** opens:

- **Days per week:** 1 to 7. It is 3 at first.
- **Daily minimum:** **Off**, or 5 to 120 minutes in steps of 5. It is Off at first.

<Screenshot name="practice-goal-sheet" alt="The Weekly Goal sheet with Days per week and Daily minimum" only="ios" caption="The Weekly Goal sheet." />

Changing the goal needs Pro. Without Pro the sheet shows your goal with **Unlock with Pro**, and your streak keeps counting with that goal.

How it counts:

- A day counts when you practice at least the daily minimum that day. With the minimum **Off**, a minute or more is enough. All your sessions that day add up.
- A week meets the goal when enough days count.
- The streak is the weeks in a row that met the goal. The current week doesn't break it: it joins the streak once you reach the goal, and it can't end the streak while it is still running.
- Paused time doesn't count, and [added sessions](#add-a-past-session) do.
- The streak is worked out from your sessions with your current goal, so changing the goal, or deleting sessions, can change it.

## Stats

<Platform only="ios mac">
The Practice tab shows, when no session runs:

- **Sessions:** all completed sessions
- **Total Time:** all your practice time
- **This Week:** your practice time in the calendar week, from the first day of the week in your region
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
The **Stats** card on the Practice tab shows:

- **Sessions:** all completed sessions
- **Total:** all your practice time
- **This Week:** your practice time in the calendar week, from the first day of the week in your region
- **Today:** your practice time today

With a daily minimum set, a **Daily Goal** ring at the top of the Practice tab shows how far you are today, and **Goal reached!** when you get there. The ring is hidden while a session runs. Set the minimum in **Weekly Goal**.
</Platform>

## Practice reminders

Makonome can remind you to practice every day.

1. Open **Settings → Practice Reminders**.
2. Turn on **<Platform only="ios mac" inline>Daily practice reminder</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>Daily Reminder</Platform>**. Allow notifications when asked.
3. Set the **<Platform only="ios mac" inline>Reminder time</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>Reminder Time</Platform>**.

Every day at that time you get a notification, **Time to <Platform only="ios mac" inline>Practice!</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>practice!</Platform>**.

<Platform only="ios mac">
If notifications are off for Makonome, Settings shows **Notifications disabled**, with a button to open the system settings and turn them on.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android the reminder starts at 9:00. If you don't allow notifications, the switch stays off. To allow them later, open your phone's settings, then Apps → Makonome → Notifications.
</Platform>

The **Practice Stats** widget shows your practice this week and your week streak. It is free. Tap it to open the Practice tab.

| Widget | Shows |
|---|---|
| Small | This week's time, the number of sessions and your streak |
| Medium | This week's time and the number of sessions, with your streak next to them |

The streak shows like **3 wks in a row**. With no streak it says **Start a streak**.
</Platform>

### The widget shows 0

<Platform only="android">
On Android the widget shows 0 at the start of each week, and until your first session. The rest of this section is about iPhone, iPad and Mac.
</Platform>

After you update to Makonome 1.1, **open the app once**. Until 1.1 the widget could not read your practice data. The first launch of 1.1 moves your data to where the widget can read it. Until then the widget shows zeros.

## Back up your practice

<Platform only="ios mac">
Your sessions, stats, streak and weekly goal can be backed up to iCloud with the rest of your Makonome data, and come back when you reinstall the app. Turn it on in **Settings → Data Management → Back up to iCloud**. See [Settings, backup and data](/manual/settings-and-data/).
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
There is no iCloud backup on Android. Android's own backup keeps your practice history with the rest of your Makonome data. See [Settings, backup and data](/manual/settings-and-data/#icloud-backup-and-restore).
</Platform>
