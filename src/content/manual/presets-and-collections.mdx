A **preset** saves your metronome setup under a name, so you can get it back in one tap. A **collection** is a list of presets, like a practice routine or a set list. You can **play a collection**: the presets come one after another, and you switch with one tap, a key or a foot pedal.

<Pro /> Loading, favoriting and deleting presets and collections is free. Creating, editing and duplicating them, and playing a collection, need Makonome Pro.

## Open the presets list

On the metronome screen, tap the list button in the top right corner. The **Presets** screen opens. Use the switch at the top to go between **Presets** and **Collections**.

## Save a preset

Set up the metronome first: tempo, time signature, subdivision and beat accents. Then:

1. Open the presets list and tap **+** in the top right corner.
2. Type a **Preset Name**.
3. Add **Notes** if you like, for example "Rudiments, loose wrists". Notes are optional.
4. Check **Current Settings**: this is what the preset saves.
5. Tap **Save**.

<Screenshot name="preset-new" alt="The New Preset form with the name My Preset, an empty Notes field, and Current Settings: BPM 120, Time Signature 4/4, Subdivision Quarter Notes, Accented Beats 1, and a Save button" caption="A new preset saves the current settings." />

A preset saves:

- The tempo
- The time signature, with its beat unit (for example dotted beats in 6/8) and accent groups
- The subdivision
- Which beats are accented and which are muted
- The name and notes

A preset does not save the sound, volume, trainers, count-in or auto stop. Those stay as they are when you load a preset.

## Load a preset

Tap the green load button on the right of the preset. Makonome goes back to the metronome screen with the preset's settings. If the metronome is playing, it switches at once and starts again at bar 1.

Each row shows the tempo, meter and subdivision, the accented and muted beats (for example **Beats 1, 3, 5 accented**), and the notes.

## Favorites

Tap the star on the left of a preset to make it a favorite. Favorites show in a **Favorites** section at the top, above **All Presets**. Tap the star again to remove it.

## Edit, duplicate and delete

- **Edit:** swipe right on a preset, or touch and hold it and pick **Edit**. You can change the name and the notes. The settings are shown but you can't change them here: to change them, load the preset, change the metronome, and save a new preset.
- **Duplicate:** touch and hold a preset and pick **Duplicate**. The copy is called "(Copy)", for example "Rock 120 (Copy)", and sits under the original.
- **Delete:** swipe left on a preset, or touch and hold it and pick **Delete**. It is deleted at once, with no question, and removed from every collection.

The menu also has **Favorite** or **Unfavorite**. Without Pro, the **+** button shows a sparkle icon and opens the upgrade screen, and Edit and Duplicate are not offered. Your saved presets keep working: you can still load, favorite and delete them.

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac, right-click a preset to see Edit, Duplicate, Favorite and Delete.
</Platform>

### Presets from a newer version

If you use Makonome on more than one device, a newer version of the app may save a preset that an older version can't read. The older version keeps it safe and shows a row **Made with a newer version of Makonome. Update the app to use it.** You can't load or edit it there, only delete it. Update the app to use it. Collections skip these presets when they play.

## Collections

To see your collections, open the presets list and pick **Collections** at the top.

<Screenshot name="collections-list" alt="The Collections screen with a Favorites section holding Daily warm-up (3 presets, Before every practice) and All Collections with Saturday gig (5 presets) and Odd meters (2 presets)" caption="Collections, with a favorite at the top." />

### Create a collection

1. In **Collections**, tap **+**.
2. Type a **Collection Name** and, if you like, **Notes**.
3. Tap **Save**.
4. Open the new collection and tap **Add Presets**.
5. Tap the presets you want, then tap **Done**.

### Inside a collection

Tap a collection to open it. You see its notes, a **Play Collection** button and its presets in order.

- **Load one preset:** tap the green load button on its row.
- **Add presets:** tap **Add Presets** at the bottom, or **••• → Add Presets** in the top right corner.
- **Remove a preset:** swipe left on it and tap **Remove**. This only takes it out of the collection. The preset stays in your presets list.
- **Reorder:** tap **••• → Reorder**, drag the presets by their handles, then tap **Done**.
- **Rename or change the notes:** tap **••• → Edit Collection**.

<Screenshot name="collection-detail" alt="The Saturday gig collection: the notes Set list, in order, a Play Collection button, 5 Presets (Rock 120, Groove 96, Odd time 7/8, Shuffle 108, Ballad 64) each with a load button, and Add Presets at the bottom" caption="A collection with its Play Collection button." />

<Screenshot name="collection-reorder" alt="The Saturday gig collection in reorder mode, with drag handles on the right of each preset and a Done button in the top right corner" only="ios" caption="Drag the handles to change the order." />

In the collections list, favorites, duplicating, editing and deleting work like they do for presets: the star, swipe right to edit, swipe left to delete, and touch and hold (right-click on Mac) for **Duplicate**, **Edit**, **Favorite** and **Delete**. Deleting a collection does not delete its presets.

## Play a collection

Playing a collection turns it into a set list. The metronome screen shows a player bar, and you go from one preset to the next with one tap, a key or a foot pedal. This is handy for a gig or a practice routine, when your hands are busy.

To play a collection:

1. Open the collection.
2. Tap **Play Collection**.
3. Makonome loads the first preset and goes back to the metronome screen. Press play.

If the metronome was already playing, it switches to the first preset at once.

<Screenshot name="collection-player-bar" alt="The metronome playing Groove 96 at 96 BPM, with the player bar above the dial: Saturday gig, Groove 96, 2 of 5, a back button on the left, a forward button and a close button on the right" caption="The player bar on the metronome screen." />

The player bar shows the collection name, the current preset, and where you are, for example **2 of 5**. It has three buttons:

- **◀◀** goes to the previous preset.
- **▶▶** goes to the next preset.
- **✕** ends the collection. The current preset stays loaded.

When you switch, the new preset's tempo, meter, subdivision and accents load at once. If the metronome plays, it starts again at bar 1 of the new preset, with no count-in. The Gap Trainer and the Tempo Changer start over too.

At the first preset the back button is off, and at the last preset the forward button is off. The collection does not go round to the start.

You can stop and start the metronome as often as you like. The collection stays on the same preset until you switch or end it.

### Keys and foot pedals

You can switch presets without touching the screen. Bluetooth page-turner foot pedals work too: most of them send the arrow keys or Page Up and Page Down, like a keyboard.

<Platform only="ios">
With a keyboard or a foot pedal connected to your iPhone or iPad:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> | Previous preset |
| <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Page Down</kbd> | Next preset |
</Platform>

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> | Previous preset |
| <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Page Down</kbd> | Next preset |

The **Metronome** menu in the menu bar has the same commands: **Previous Preset**, **Next Preset** and **Stop Playing Collection**. They are on only while a collection plays.
</Platform>

The keys only work while the player bar is on screen. They do nothing while you type (for example a tempo) or while a sheet is open over the metronome.

<Screenshot name="collection-player-bar-ipad" alt="The metronome on iPad playing a collection, with the player bar above the dial showing Saturday gig, Groove 96, 2 of 5" only="ios" caption="On iPad, a keyboard or a foot pedal switches the presets." />

### What ends the collection

The collection ends, and the player bar goes away, when you:

- Tap **✕** on the player bar, or pick **Stop Playing Collection** on Mac
- Load a preset by hand, from the presets list or a collection
- Start a [practice session](/manual/practice-tracking/)
- Delete the collection, or remove all its presets
- Reset all data in Settings
- Close Makonome completely. The collection is not saved, so it is gone the next time you open the app
- Lose Pro, when the trial ends without a purchase

If you edit, reorder or delete presets while a collection plays, the player picks up the change at the next switch.

While a Free Time run plays in the [Accuracy Trainer](/manual/accuracy-trainer/#free-time), the player bar's buttons and keys are off, so the run is not interrupted.

With VoiceOver on, Makonome reads the preset name and its place, for example "Groove 96. Preset 2 of 5".
