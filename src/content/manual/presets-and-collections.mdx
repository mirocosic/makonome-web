A **preset** saves your metronome setup under a name, so you can get it back in one tap. A **collection** is a list of presets, like a practice routine or a set list. You can **play a collection**: the presets come one after another, and you switch with one tap, a key or a foot pedal.

<Pro /> Loading, favoriting, deleting and reordering presets and collections is free, and so are searching and sorting presets. Creating, editing and duplicating them, adding presets to a collection and playing a collection need Makonome Pro.

## Save a preset

Set up the metronome first: tempo, time signature, subdivision and beat accents. Then:

<Platform only="ios mac">
1. Open the presets list and tap **+** in the top right corner.
2. Type a **Preset Name**.
3. Add an **Artist** and **Notes** if you like, for example "Rudiments, loose wrists". Both are optional.
4. Check **Current Settings**: this is what the preset saves.
5. Tap **Save**.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
1. Open the presets list and tap the round **+** button in the bottom right corner.
2. In **Save as Preset**, type a **Name**.
3. Add **Artist (optional)** and **Notes (optional)** if you like, for example "Rudiments, loose wrists".
4. Check **Current Settings**: this is what the preset saves.
5. Tap **Save**.
</Platform>

<Screenshot name="preset-new" alt="The New Preset form with the name My Preset, empty Artist and Notes fields, and Current Settings: BPM 120, Time Signature 4/4, Subdivision Quarter Notes, Accented Beats 1, and a Save button" caption="A new preset saves the current settings." />

A preset saves:

- The tempo
- The time signature, with its beat unit (for example dotted beats in 6/8) and accent groups
- The subdivision, with its [rests](/manual/time-signatures/#rests-inside-the-beat)
- Which beats are accented and which are muted
- The name, artist and notes

<Platform only="ios mac">
A preset does not save the sound, volume, trainers, count-in or auto stop. Those stay as they are when you load a preset.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android a preset also saves the sound. Loading a preset sets its sound. A preset does not save the volume, pitch, trainers, count-in or auto stop: those stay as they are.
</Platform>

## Load a preset

Tap <Platform only="ios mac" inline>the green load button on the right of the preset</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>the preset's row</Platform>. Makonome goes back to the metronome screen with the preset's settings. If the metronome is playing, it switches at once and starts again at bar 1.

Each row shows the name and artist, the tempo, meter and subdivision (with dots for the rests, if it has any), the accented and muted beats (for example **Beats 1, 3, 5 accented**), and the notes.

## Favorites

Tap the star on the <Platform only="ios mac" inline>left</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>right</Platform> of a preset to make it a favorite. Favorites show in a **Favorites** section at the top, above **All Presets**. Tap the star again to remove it.

## Find and sort presets

**Search:** type in the **Name or artist** field at the top of the Presets screen. It finds presets by name or artist, and doesn't mind upper or lower case or accents.<Platform only="ios mac" inline> It shows only on Presets. If you switch to Collections, your text is kept for when you come back.</Platform>

**Sort:** tap the sort button <Platform only="ios mac" inline>in the top right corner</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>next to the search field</Platform> and pick one under **Sort By**:

- **Manual:** your own order. This is the order at first.
- **Name:** A to Z.
- **Recently Played:** the presets you played last come first.
- **Recently Created:** the newest presets come first.

Makonome remembers your choice. Favorites stay in their own section at the top, sorted the same way.

### Reorder presets

Dragging presets into your own order is free. It works in **Manual** sort with an empty search and at least two presets.

<Platform only="ios mac">
1. Tap the sort button and pick **Reorder**.
2. Drag the presets by their handles.
3. Tap **Done**.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
Drag a preset by the ≡ handle on its left. With TalkBack, use the **Move up** and **Move down** actions.
</Platform>

A preset moves only inside its own section: **Favorites** or **All Presets**.

## Edit, duplicate and delete

<Platform only="ios mac">
- **Edit:** swipe right on a preset, or touch and hold it and pick **Edit**. You can change the name, the artist and the notes. The settings are shown but you can't change them here: to change them, load the preset, change the metronome, and save a new preset.
- **Duplicate:** touch and hold a preset and pick **Duplicate**. The copy is called "(Copy)", for example "Rock 120 (Copy)", and sits under the original.
- **Delete:** swipe left on a preset, or touch and hold it and pick **Delete**. It is deleted at once, with no question, and removed from every collection.

The menu also has **Favorite** or **Unfavorite**. Without Pro, the **+** button shows a sparkle icon and opens the upgrade screen, and Edit and Duplicate are not offered. Your saved presets keep working: you can still load, favorite and delete them.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android, swipe left on a preset to show two buttons:

- **Edit** (the pencil): change the name, the artist and the notes. To change the settings, load the preset, change the metronome, and save a new preset.
- **Delete** (the bin): the preset is deleted at once, with no question, and removed from every collection.

To duplicate a preset, touch and hold it (or right-click it) and pick **Duplicate**. The copy is called "(Copy)", for example "Rock 120 (Copy)", and sits under the original. The menu also has **Favorite** or **Unfavorite**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

Editing and duplicating need Pro. Without Pro, the **+** button opens the upgrade screen, and a swipe shows only Delete. Your saved presets keep working: you can still load, favorite and delete them.
</Platform>

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac, right-click a preset to see Edit, Duplicate, Favorite and Delete.
</Platform>

### Presets from a newer version

If you use Makonome on more than one device, a newer version of the app may save a preset that an older version can't read. The older version keeps it safe and shows a row **Made with a newer version of Makonome. Update the app to use it.** You can't load or edit it there, only delete it. Update the app to use it. Collections skip these presets when they play.

A preset with quintuplets, sextuplets, septuplets or rests works differently. An older version loads it, but plays it with no subdivision. The pattern stays in the preset, and the newer version plays it again. If you change the preset's subdivision in the older version, the pattern is gone.

<Platform only="android">
This is about iPhone, iPad and Mac. Android has no iCloud backup, so it doesn't get presets from other devices.
</Platform>

## Collections

To see your collections, open the presets list and pick **Collections** at the top.

<Screenshot name="collections-list" alt="The Collections screen with a Favorites section holding Daily warm-up (3 presets, Before every practice) and All Collections with Saturday gig (5 presets) and Odd meters (2 presets)" caption="Collections, with a favorite at the top." />

### Create a collection

<Platform only="ios mac">
1. In **Collections**, tap **+**.
2. Type a **Collection Name** and, if you like, **Notes**.
3. Tap **Save**.
4. Open the new collection and tap **Add Presets**.
5. Tap the presets you want, then tap **Done**.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
1. In **Collections**, tap the round **+** button.
2. In **New Collection**, type a **Name** and, if you like, **Notes (optional)**.
3. Tap **Save**.
4. Open the new collection and tap **Add Presets**.
5. Tick the presets you want, then tap **Done**.
</Platform>

### Inside a collection

Tap a collection to open it. You see its notes, a **Play Collection** button and its presets in order.

<Platform only="ios mac">
- **Load one preset:** tap the green load button on its row.
- **Add presets:** tap **Add Presets** at the bottom, or **••• → Add Presets** in the top right corner.
- **Remove a preset:** swipe left on it and tap **Remove from Collection**. This only takes it out of the collection. The preset stays in your presets list.
- **Reorder:** tap **••• → Reorder**, drag the presets by their handles, then tap **Done**.
- **Rename or change the notes:** tap **••• → Edit Collection**.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
- **Load one preset:** tap its row.
- **Add presets:** tap **Add Presets** at the bottom, tick the presets you want, then tap **Done**. Favorites come first, and presets already in the collection stay ticked.
- **Remove a preset:** tap **−** on its row. This only takes it out of the collection. The preset stays in your presets list.
- **Reorder:** drag a preset by the handle on its left. With TalkBack, use the **Move up** and **Move down** actions.
- **Rename or change the notes:** tap the pencil in the top bar.
</Platform>

## Play a collection

Playing a collection turns it into a set list. The metronome screen shows a player bar, and you go from one preset to the next with one tap, a key or a foot pedal. This is handy for a gig or a practice routine, when your hands are busy.

To play a collection:

1. Open the collection.
2. Tap **Play Collection**.
3. Makonome loads the first preset and goes back to the metronome screen. Press play.

If the metronome was already playing, it switches to the first preset at once.

<Screenshot name="collection-player-bar" alt="The metronome playing Groove 96 at 96 BPM, with the player bar above the dial: Saturday gig, Groove 96, 2 of 5, a round back button on the left, a round forward button and a small close button on the right" caption="The player bar on the metronome screen." />

<Screenshot name="collection-player-bar" alt="The metronome on Android playing at 120 BPM with the readout Bar 2, Beat 2, and the player bar above the buttons: Daily practice, Odd time study, 2 of 4, with back, forward and close buttons" only="android" caption="The player bar on Android." />

The player bar shows the collection name, the current preset, and where you are, for example **2 of 5**. It has three buttons:

- **◀◀** goes to the previous preset.
- **▶▶** goes to the next preset.
- **✕** ends the collection. The current preset stays loaded.

When you switch, the new preset's tempo, meter, subdivision and accents load at once. If the metronome plays, it starts again at bar 1 of the new preset, with no count-in. The Gap Trainer and the Tempo Changer start over too.

At the first preset the back button is off, and at the last preset the forward button is off. The collection does not go round to the start.

You can stop and start the metronome as often as you like. The collection stays on the same preset until you switch or end it.

### Keys and foot pedals

You can switch presets without touching the screen. Bluetooth page-turner foot pedals work too: most of them send the arrow keys or Page Up and Page Down, like a keyboard.

<Platform only="ios android">
With a keyboard or a foot pedal connected to your <Platform only="ios" inline>iPhone or iPad</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>phone or tablet</Platform>:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> | Previous preset |
| <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Page Down</kbd> | Next preset |
</Platform>

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> | Previous preset |
| <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Page Down</kbd> | Next preset |

The **Metronome** menu in the menu bar has the same commands: **Previous Preset**, **Next Preset** and **Stop Playing Collection**. They are on only while a collection plays.
</Platform>

<Platform only="ios mac">
The keys only work while the player bar is on screen. They do nothing while you type (for example a tempo) or while a sheet is open over the metronome.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android the keys work on any screen of the app while a collection plays, but not while a sheet or a box is open. While you type in a text field, the arrow keys move the cursor, and Page Up and Page Down still switch presets. Holding a key down moves one preset only.
</Platform>

### What ends the collection

The collection ends, and the player bar goes away, when you:

- Tap **✕** on the player bar, or pick **Stop Playing Collection** on Mac
- Load a preset by hand, from the presets list or a collection
- Start a [practice session](/manual/practice-tracking/)
- Delete the collection, or remove all its presets
- Reset all data in Settings
- Close Makonome completely. The collection is not saved, so it is gone the next time you open the app
- Lose Pro, when the trial ends without a purchase

If you edit, reorder or delete presets while a collection plays, the player picks up the change at the next switch.

<Platform only="ios mac">
While a Free Time run plays in the [Accuracy Trainer](/manual/accuracy-trainer/#free-time), the player bar's buttons and keys are off, so the run is not interrupted.
</Platform>

With <Platform only="ios mac" inline>VoiceOver</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>TalkBack</Platform> on, Makonome reads the preset name and its place, for example "Groove 96. Preset 2 of 5".
