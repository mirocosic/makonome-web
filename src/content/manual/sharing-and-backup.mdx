Makonome can put your presets and collections in a **Makonome file** (`.makonome`). Send one to a bandmate or a student, and they open it in Makonome to add what's in it. A **backup file** holds everything: presets, collections, practice history and settings. Use it to move to a new device, also from iPhone to Android and back.

A file made on iPhone, iPad or Mac opens on Android, and a file made on Android opens on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

<Pro /> Sharing and importing presets and collections need Makonome Pro, like presets themselves. Backing up to a file and restoring a backup are free.

## Share presets or collections

1. On the metronome screen, tap the list button to open **Presets**.
2. Pick **Presets** or **Collections** at the top.
3. Tap <Platform only="ios mac" inline>**•••** in the top right corner</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>**⋮** in the top right corner</Platform> and pick **Share Presets…** or **Share Collections…**.
4. Tick the ones you want to send. **Select All** ticks them all.
5. Tap **Share 3 Presets** (or however many you picked) at the bottom.
6. Pick where to send the file: Messages, Mail, <Platform only="ios mac" inline>AirDrop, </Platform>Drive and so on.

All your picks go in one file. A collection comes with its presets, so the person you send it to gets the whole setlist.

To send just one, touch and hold a preset or a collection<Platform only="mac" inline> (or right-click it)</Platform> and pick **<Platform only="ios mac" inline>Share…</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>Share</Platform>**. Inside a collection you can also share it from <Platform only="ios mac" inline>**••• → Share…**</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>the share button in the top bar</Platform>.

<Platform only="mac">
On Mac, **Export…** sits next to Share in the same menus. It saves the file to a folder you pick. You can also drag a preset or a collection out of the list onto the desktop or into a Finder window.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android, **Save to…** in the touch-and-hold menu saves the file to a folder you pick, for example in Drive.
</Platform>

A file has each preset's name, artist, notes, tempo, time signature, accent groups, subdivision with its rests, beat accents and sound. It doesn't have your favorites or when you last played a preset. The file is named after the preset or collection, like `Rock 120.makonome`, or `Makonome presets.makonome` when it holds several.

<Platform only="ios mac">
Without Pro, **Share Presets…** and **Share Collections…** open the upgrade screen, and the touch-and-hold menus have no Share.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
Without Pro, **Share Presets…** and **Share Collections…** open the upgrade screen, and the touch-and-hold menus have no Share or Save to….
</Platform>

## Import a file

To add the presets and collections from a Makonome file:

- **Open the file.** Tap it in <Platform only="ios" inline>Messages, Mail, Files or AirDrop</Platform><Platform only="mac" inline>Mail, Messages or Finder</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>your mail or chat app, or in Files</Platform>, and choose Makonome.
- **Or pick it in Makonome.** In **Presets**, tap <Platform only="ios mac" inline>**••• → Import…**</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>**⋮ → Import**</Platform> and pick the file.

Makonome shows what's in the file first, for example **Import Collection** with the collection and its presets, and where it came from: **From Makonome 1.4.0 on Android, 11 Nov 2026**. Nothing changes until you tap **Import**. Tap **Cancel** to leave it.

- Imported presets and collections are **new copies**, added at the end of your lists. Nothing you already have is changed or replaced.
- If you already have a preset or collection with the same name, the new one gets a number, like **Money (2)**.
- A file from a newer version of Makonome still imports. If it uses something this version doesn't have, the preview says **Some settings were simplified for this version.**

<Platform only="ios mac">
If the file isn't a Makonome file, Makonome says **Can't Open File** and **This isn't a Makonome file.** Files over 10 MB don't open.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
If the file isn't a Makonome file, Makonome says **Can't Import** and **This isn't a Makonome file.** Files over 10 MB don't open.
</Platform>

Anyone can open a file to see what's in it. Tapping **Import** needs Pro.

## Back up to a file

A backup file holds everything you need on a new device:

- All presets, with your favorites
- All collections
- Your finished practice sessions, with their notes
- Your settings: sound, volume, pitch, count-in, auto stop, screen flash, Fine-grained BPM, theme, preset sort order, Auto-start metronome, your goals and the practice reminder

To make one:

1. Open **Settings → <Platform only="ios mac" inline>Data Management</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>Data</Platform>**.
2. Tap **Back Up to File**.
3. <Platform only="ios" inline>Pick where to keep it, for example **Save to Files** or iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself.</Platform><Platform only="mac" inline>Pick a folder and click **Save**.</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>Pick where to keep it, for example Drive, or send it to yourself.</Platform>

The file is called `Makonome backup` with the date and time, for example `Makonome backup 2026-11-18 14-30.makonome`. It is free.

<Platform only="ios mac">
This is separate from [iCloud backup](/manual/settings-and-data/#icloud-backup-and-restore), which runs by itself. Use a backup file to keep a copy you control, or to move to Android.
</Platform>

## Restore from a backup

1. On the new device, open **Settings → <Platform only="ios mac" inline>Data Management</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>Data</Platform> → Restore from Backup**.
2. Pick the backup file. You can also just open the file.
3. **Restore Backup** shows how many presets, collections and practice sessions are new, and how many are already here.
4. Turn **Settings** on if you want the backup's sound, playback, goal and reminder settings to replace this device's. On a device with no presets and no sessions yet, it is on at first.
5. Tap **Restore**.

A restore only adds what's missing. Presets, collections and sessions already on the device stay exactly as they are, so restoring the same file twice adds nothing the second time. If everything in the file is already here and Settings is off, Makonome says so and there's nothing to restore.

Restoring is free. Without Pro, the settings that need Pro (Pro sounds, pitch and the weekly goal) stay as they are.

## Move between iPhone and Android

1. On the old device, make a [backup file](#back-up-to-a-file) and send it to yourself, or put it in a cloud drive.
2. Install Makonome on the new device and open the file there, or use **Restore from Backup**.
3. Restore it with **Settings** on.

Your Pro purchase doesn't move with the file: Pro on iPhone, iPad and Mac and Pro on Android are separate purchases. See [The free trial and Pro](/manual/getting-started/#the-free-trial-and-pro).
