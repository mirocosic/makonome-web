<Platform only="ios mac">
You can control Makonome with **Siri**, build **Shortcuts** with it, and find your presets and collections in **Spotlight**, without opening the app. The metronome plays in the background.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
On Android 17 and later, **AI assistants** can use Makonome through Android's App Functions: start and stop the metronome, play a preset, read your practice history, and make presets and setlists for you.
</Platform>

<Pro /> Playing, stopping, setting the tempo, playing a preset and asking about your practice are free. Playing a collection, creating presets, adding presets to collections and starting a practice session need Makonome Pro, the same as in the app. Without Pro, <Platform only="ios mac" inline>Siri</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>the assistant</Platform> tells you that, for example **Presets & Collections needs Makonome Pro.**

## Siri

Say a phrase, or tap a Makonome shortcut in the Shortcuts app. Most phrases end in "in Makonome", for example "Start the metronome in Makonome".

| What it does | Say | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Start the metronome | "Start Makonome", or "Start the metronome in Makonome". You can give a tempo. | |
| Stop the metronome | "Stop Makonome", or "Stop the metronome in Makonome" | |
| Set the tempo | "Set the tempo in Makonome". Siri asks **What tempo?** If the metronome plays, it keeps playing at the new tempo. | |
| Play a preset | "Play Warm-up 80 in Makonome", or "Start a preset in Makonome" | |
| Play a collection | "Play the collection Saturday gig in Makonome". It starts on the first preset. | <Pro /> |
| Create a preset | "Create a preset in Makonome", with a name, tempo, time signature (4/4 if you don't say) and artist | <Pro /> |
| Add a preset to a collection | "Add a preset to a collection in Makonome" | <Pro /> |
| Start a practice session | "Start practicing in Makonome". Siri asks **For how many minutes?** (say 0 for no goal). You can pick a preset. | <Pro /> |
| Ask about your practice | "How much did I practice in Makonome", or "Show my practice this week in Makonome" | |

The tempo goes from 20 to 400 BPM, the same as the dial. In other meters, like 6/8, it follows the dial too.

Siri answers short, for example **Playing at 120 BPM.** or **You practiced 1 hour 5 minutes on 3 days this week.** with your week streak, if you have one.

## Shortcuts

Every action above is also in the **Shortcuts** app, under **Makonome**. Use them in your own shortcuts, for example a "Practice" shortcut that starts a 30-minute session with your warm-up preset, or put one on the Home Screen.

## Spotlight

Your presets and collections show up when you search with Spotlight. A preset shows its tempo, time signature and artist, for example **120 BPM · 4/4 · Rock band**. A collection shows how many presets it has.

- Tap a **preset** to open the metronome with it loaded. It doesn't start playing.
- Tap a **collection** to open it in the [collection player](/manual/presets-and-collections/#play-a-collection), on its first preset. That needs Pro: without Pro, Makonome just opens.

</Platform>

## AI assistants on Android

AI assistants that support App Functions can use Makonome on Android 17 and later. Makonome doesn't name a specific assistant: which ones work depends on your phone and the assistant.

The switch is **Settings → General → Let AI Assistants Use Makonome**. It is on at first. Turn it off and no assistant can use Makonome. The switch shows only on phones with Android 17 or later.

With the switch on, an assistant can see your presets, collections and practice history, notes included. It can:

| What | Pro |
|---|---|
| Start the metronome at a tempo, or with a preset | |
| Start a collection | <Pro /> |
| Stop the metronome | |
| Find presets and collections, and read their settings | |
| Read your practice sessions and a practice summary for a time range | |
| Create or change a preset | <Pro /> |
| Create or change a collection | <Pro /> |
| Make a setlist from a list of songs, as a new collection | <Pro /> |

Tempos go from 20 to 400 BPM. An assistant can't delete anything.

If Makonome can't play, the assistant is told **Makonome's metronome couldn't be reached. Ask the user to open Makonome.**

</Platform>
