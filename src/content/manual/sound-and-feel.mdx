## Sounds and settings

Tap the speaker icon on the main screen to choose your click sound:

- **Click sounds:** Click, Clave, Woodblock, Cowbell, Voice Count
- **Pitch adjustment:** Raise or lower the click pitch
- **Volume:** Tap the speaker icon on the main screen
- **Haptic feedback:** Feel the beat (experimental feature)
