## Gap Trainer

The Gap Trainer helps you develop your internal sense of time by periodically muting the metronome.

Access it from the **⋯** menu and configure:

- **Normal bars:** How many bars the metronome plays
- **Muted bars:** How many bars of silence follow

The pattern repeats: play → silence → play → silence. Try to keep steady time during the silent sections!

### Progressive Mode

Enable Progressive Mode to make the gaps automatically grow longer each cycle. Configure:

- **Add Bars:** How many extra muted bars to add each cycle (1-4)
- **Max Muted:** The maximum number of muted bars before the pattern resets

This gradually increases the challenge as you practice, helping you build confidence with longer silences.

## Tempo Changer

Gradually increase tempo over time to push your limits progressively.

Access it from the **⋯** menu and configure:

- **BPM Increment:** How much to increase (1-10 BPM)
- **Interval Mode:** Increase every N bars, or every N seconds
- **Target BPM:** Optionally set a maximum tempo to stop at

Great for building speed on exercises or working up to a target tempo.
