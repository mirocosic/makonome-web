Makonome has two trainers. The **Gap Trainer** takes the click away for some bars or seconds, so you learn to keep time on your own. The **Tempo Changer** makes the tempo faster or slower as you play, so you can build speed or cool down step by step.

<Pro /> Both trainers need Makonome Pro. You find both in the **•••** menu<Platform only="android" inline> (⋮ on Android)</Platform> on the metronome screen. Their settings stay the same when you load a preset: they are not part of presets.

## Gap Trainer

The Gap Trainer drops the beat on purpose. In a gap the click is gone and the beat goes dark, so only your own timing is left. Keep playing through the silence. When the click comes back, you hear at once if you were still in time.

There are two modes:

- **Pattern:** a number of bars with the click, then a number of bars without it, and repeat.
- **Timed:** a number of seconds with the click, then a number of seconds without it, and repeat.

To set up the Gap Trainer:

1. On the metronome screen, tap **••• → Gap Trainer**.
2. Turn on the **Gap Trainer** switch at the top.
3. Pick **Pattern** or **Timed**.
4. For Pattern, set **Normal Bars** (1 to 16) and **Muted Bars** (1 to 16). For Timed, set **On** and **Off** (1 to 60 seconds each).
5. Close the sheet and press play.

The line <Platform only="ios mac" inline>under the switch</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>at the bottom of the sheet</Platform> sums up what you set, for example **4 normal → 4 muted → repeat** or **10 s on, 5 s off**.

<Platform only="android">
On Android the switch at the top is called **Enabled**, and you set the bars and seconds with − and +.
</Platform>

### Progressive mode

In progressive mode, a Pattern option, the silence gets longer each time. Use it to stretch how long you can keep time alone.

1. In the Gap Trainer sheet, turn on **Progressive Mode**<Platform only="ios mac" inline> ("Gaps grow longer each cycle")</Platform>.
2. **Starting Muted**<Platform only="ios mac" inline> (the Muted Bars wheel)</Platform> sets the first gap.
3. **Add Bars** (+1 to +4) sets how many muted bars are added after each cycle.
4. **Max Muted** (2 to 32) sets the longest gap.

For example, 4 normal bars, starting with 2 muted, +1, max 8: the gaps are 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 bars, then they stay at 8. When you start the metronome again, the gaps start at 2 again.

<Platform only="android">
On Android the settings are called **Starting Muted Bars**, **Add Bars per Cycle** and **Max Muted Bars**.
</Platform>

## Tempo Changer

The Tempo Changer changes the tempo by a few BPM after a number of bars or seconds. Start slow and finish faster, or the other way round, without stopping to turn the dial.

To set up the Tempo Changer:

1. On the metronome screen, tap **••• → Tempo Changer**.
2. Turn on the **Tempo Changer** switch at the top.
3. Pick **Faster** or **Slower**.
4. Pick **Bars** or **Time**.
5. Set **BPM Change** (1 to 10): how much each step changes the tempo.
6. Set how often it changes: **Bar Interval** (1 to 20 bars), or with Time, **Seconds** (5 to 120 seconds).
7. To go to a set tempo, turn on **Target BPM** and pick the tempo (20 to 400, in steps of <Platform only="ios mac" inline>10</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>1</Platform>). Then pick what happens there: **Stay**, **Restart** or **Bounce**. See [At the target](#at-the-target).
8. Close the sheet, set your start tempo on the dial, and press play.

The line <Platform only="ios mac" inline>under the switch</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>at the bottom of the sheet</Platform> sums it up, for example **<Platform only="ios mac" inline>Increase by 2 BPM every 4 bars until 160 BPM, then restart</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>+2 BPM every 4 bars until 160 BPM, then restart</Platform>**.

<Platform only="android">
On Android the switch at the top is called **Enabled**. **Faster** and **Slower** are under **Direction**, **Bars** and **Time** are under **Interval Mode**, and **BPM Change** is a slider. The bar interval is called **Every N Bars**, with Time you pick the seconds under **Interval**, the target tempo is called **Target**, and **Stay**, **Restart** and **Bounce** are under **At Target**.
</Platform>

### While the Tempo Changer plays

- The tempo changes by the BPM Change at each step. The dial follows, so you always see the current tempo.
- <Platform only="ios mac" inline>A **Tempo+** or **Tempo−** tag shows in the line above the dial while the Tempo Changer is on.</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>The ⋮ button is highlighted while the Tempo Changer is on.</Platform>
- Under the bar and beat readout, a line counts down to the next step: **Tempo +2 in 3 bars**, **Tempo −2 in 1 bar**, or with Time, **<Platform only="ios mac" inline>Tempo +2 every 30s</Platform><Platform only="android" inline>+2 BPM every 30s</Platform>**.
- When you stop the metronome, the dial stays at the tempo you reached. Set the start tempo again before the next run.

### At the target

With **Target BPM** on, the tempo changes until it gets to the target. A step never goes past the target: if it would, the tempo goes to the target exactly. If the target is on the wrong side, for example above the start tempo with Slower on, the first step goes straight to the target.

What happens then depends on your choice:

- **Stay:** the metronome keeps playing at the target, and the line shows **Target reached: 160 BPM<Platform only="ios mac" inline> 👍</Platform>**.
- **Restart:** after one more interval at the target, the tempo jumps back to the start tempo and changes again from there.
- **Bounce:** after one more interval at the target, the tempo steps back to the start tempo, holds it for one interval, and goes again. It keeps going until you stop the metronome.

The start tempo is where the run began. If you change the tempo by hand while the metronome plays, that tempo becomes the new start.

<Platform only="ios mac">
**Stay**, **Restart** and **Bounce** are always in the sheet. They are dimmed while **Target BPM** is off, and your choice is kept.
</Platform>

<Platform only="android">
**Stay**, **Restart** and **Bounce** are always in the sheet. You can't pick one while **Target BPM** is off.
</Platform>

Without a target, the tempo keeps changing until it gets to the end of the range, 400 BPM going faster or 20 BPM going slower, and stays there.

### Compound meters

In meters with a dotted beat, like 6/8, the BPM Change and the target count dotted beats, like the dial. The top of the range is still 400 eighth notes a minute (133 dotted beats). See [Compound meters](/manual/time-signatures/#compound-meters-and-the-dotted-pulse).

## Using both together

You can turn on the Gap Trainer and the Tempo Changer at the same time. The tempo keeps changing in the gaps too, so when the click comes back it may be faster or slower.

## With count-in, auto stop and collections

- **Count-in:** the count-in plays first, at the start tempo. The Gap Trainer starts with its normal bars after it, Timed starts its On seconds after it, and the Tempo Changer starts counting after it.
- **Auto stop:** gaps count. 16 bars means 16 bars of click and silence together.
- **Loading a preset while playing:** the run starts again at bar 1. The Gap Trainer goes back to its first normal bar (and its first gap in progressive mode), and the Tempo Changer starts counting again from the new preset's tempo. The same happens when you go to the next or previous preset in a [playing collection](/manual/presets-and-collections/#play-a-collection).
- **Accuracy Trainer Free Time:** the Tempo Changer pauses while a Free Time run plays. See [Accuracy Trainer](/manual/accuracy-trainer/#free-time).

See [Count-in and auto stop](/manual/count-in-and-auto-stop/) for more.
